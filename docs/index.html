The world's biggest stories, explained in 60 seconds.

World in 60 is an English-language news channel. Two cartoon anchors, Sam and Nina, explain the day's most important international stories in short videos — with the key numbers, the context, and why it matters.

  • Every story is cross-checked against at least two independent news outlets.
  • Sources are listed in every video description.
  • Videos are produced with the help of AI and reviewed against the original reporting.
