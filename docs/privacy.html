Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

World in 60 ("we") runs an automated publishing tool that posts our own news videos to our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

What data the tool accesses

The tool connects only to the World in 60 accounts owned by the channel operator, using the official APIs of YouTube (Google), Instagram (Meta) and TikTok. It uses this access solely to upload videos, set their titles and descriptions, and read basic upload status.

It does not access, collect, store or share any personal data of viewers or of any other user. It does not use cookies or analytics on this website.

How access tokens are stored

Access tokens for our own accounts are stored encrypted and are used only by our publishing tool. They are never sold or shared with third parties.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions or the equivalent settings of Instagram and TikTok.
